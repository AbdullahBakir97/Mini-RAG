Remote work

Larkspur Labs is a remote-first company. Most people work from home, and the offices in Berlin and Lisbon are open to anyone who prefers to work there.

Where you can work

You can work from anywhere in the country your employment contract is based in. Working from another country is possible for up to 30 days per year, as long as you tell HR before you go; longer stays abroad need approval, because of tax and insurance rules.

Working hours

Teams agree on core hours of at least four hours a day in which everyone is available for meetings and questions, usually between 10:00 and 15:00 in the team's main time zone. Outside core hours you plan your own day. Nobody is expected to answer messages in the evening or on weekends, unless they are on call.

Home office allowance

Every employee receives a one-time home office allowance of 1,000 euros to set up a desk, a chair and good lighting, and a monthly allowance of 40 euros for internet and electricity. The one-time allowance is renewed every three years. Items bought with it belong to you.

Coworking spaces

If you live more than 50 kilometres from an office, the company pays for a desk in a coworking space near you, up to 250 euros per month. Book it through the office team rather than paying for it yourself.

Meeting in person

Each team meets in person twice a year for a week of planning and social time, and the whole company gathers once a year. Travel for these meetings follows the expenses and travel guide.

Communication

Write things down. Decisions are recorded in the team's decision log, and questions go to a channel rather than a direct message, so others can find the answer later. Video is optional in internal meetings, except for the first meeting with someone new.
