Time off and leave

Larkspur Labs wants people to rest. This guide covers vacation, public holidays, sickness and family leave.

Vacation

Everyone receives 28 vacation days per calendar year, on top of public holidays. Up to five unused days can be carried over into the next year; they must be taken by the end of March, after which they expire. Request vacation in the HR portal at least two weeks ahead for anything longer than three days, and agree the dates with your team so the on-call rotation stays covered.

Public holidays

The public holidays of the country you work from apply. Teams spread across several countries agree in advance who covers the shared services on holidays that only some of them have.

Sick leave

If you are ill, tell your manager before your usual start time and record the sick day in the HR portal. For an absence longer than three consecutive working days, upload a doctor's note. Sick days do not reduce your vacation days, and there is no fixed limit on paid sick leave.

Caring for others

Up to five paid days per year are available to care for a sick child or relative. Record them in the HR portal as care leave.

Parental leave

Birth parents receive 20 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Other parents, including adoptive parents, receive 12 weeks of fully paid leave, which can be taken in up to three blocks within the first year after the birth or adoption. Talk to HR at least three months before the leave starts, so your work can be handed over.

Sabbaticals

After five years at Larkspur Labs you can take an unpaid sabbatical of up to three months. Your position and salary are kept, and the time counts towards your years of service.

Volunteering

Each person has two paid volunteering days per year for work with a registered charity.
