Expenses and travel

This guide explains what Larkspur Labs pays for when you travel or spend money for work, and how to get it back.

Submitting expenses

Submit every expense through the finance portal with a photo or PDF of the receipt. Expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase; older receipts are only reimbursed with your manager's written approval. Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly salary.

Expenses up to 150 euros need no approval in advance. Anything more expensive needs your manager's approval before you buy it, recorded in the finance portal.

Company cards

Team leads and frequent travellers can request a company card. Card payments still need a receipt in the finance portal within 30 days; missing receipts are deducted from the next salary.

Booking travel

Book flights, trains and hotels through the travel desk at least fourteen days before the trip when possible. Trains are preferred over flights for journeys under five hours. Economy class is the default for flights; flights longer than eight hours may be booked in premium economy.

Hotels

Hotels are booked up to 180 euros per night in most cities and up to 250 euros per night in London, Paris, Zurich and New York. If a conference hotel costs more, ask the travel desk before booking.

Meals and per diem

During business trips you receive a daily allowance of 45 euros for meals, paid automatically with the expense report for the trip. Meals with customers are reimbursed separately and need the names of the attendees on the receipt.

Conferences and books

Conference tickets, workshops and books are paid from your personal learning budget, which is described in the performance and growth guide. Submit them through the finance portal like any other expense and choose the category Learning.

Things we do not pay for

Fines, including parking and speeding tickets, personal entertainment, upgrades you choose for comfort, and alcohol beyond one drink with a meal are not reimbursed.
